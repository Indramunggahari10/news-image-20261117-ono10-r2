# 17 November 2026 — The World in One Image

A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November
2026 in a single 1024x1024 image: masthead, hero illustration (a falling
bridge, a globe, a glowing chip), and eight verified headlines spanning the
Middle East, Ukraine, business, diplomacy, health, Asia, Europe and Africa.

## Headlines (all events of 17 November 2026)

1. **Middle East** — US strikes Iran missile site on Qeshm Island
2. **Ukraine** — Kerch Bridge span falls after second day of strikes
3. **Business** — Nvidia tops $115bn revenue; Huang will not return
4. **Diplomacy** — Trump heads to Shenzhen for talks with Xi, Putin
5. **Health** — US loses measles-free status after 26 years
6. **Asia** — Mongolia's president resigns amid corruption scandal
7. **Europe** — 146,000 march in France over cost of living
8. **Africa** — Ebola cases pass 11,000 in DR Congo

Sources: One Turn world-news digest for 17 Nov 2026, BBC world headlines
(17 Nov 2026), Foreign Exchanges world roundup (17 Nov 2026), Daily Baku
world desk (17 Nov 2026), and a 17 Nov morning-headlines roundup.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # writes out/entry.png
```

`main.py` is stdlib-only and deterministic: it copies the bundled
`entry.png` byte-for-byte, so two runs produce identical output.
